Move-in Cost Calculator

Short answer

Move-in cost is the cash needed on day one: first-month (or prorated) rent + security deposit + pet and admin fees + moving costs. Add each item separately; a deposit that looks small on its own can still make the first-month total much larger than one month's rent.

Last verified: 2026-10-25. This is general information, not legal advice. City ordinances may be stricter and may override state rules. Check the official source for your city and state before acting.

Formula

First-month cash needed = first-month rent (prorated amount if entered, otherwise full monthly rent) + security deposit + pet/other fees + application/admin fees.

Total move-in cost = first-month cash needed + moving costs.

Worked example

Monthly rent $1,800, prorated first month $929.03, deposit $1,800, pet fee $300, application fee $50, moving costs $400.

  • First-month cash needed: $929.03 + $1,800 + $300 + $50 = $3,079.03.
  • Total move-in cost including moving: $3,079.03 + $400 = $3,479.03.

Limitations

  • Deposit rules vary by state — some states cap deposits and set return deadlines, others do not. Use the Deposit calculator and check the official source for your state before assuming a deposit amount is lawful.
  • This calculator does not include recurring costs (utilities, insurance, parking) or costs after the first month.
  • Fees that are refundable vs. non-refundable are treated the same here for cash-needed purposes; your lease should say which is which.

Sources

  • Deposit caps and return deadlines are state-specific and are covered, where verified, on the deposit tool and in the Guides. No deposit cap is assumed by this calculator.
  • For state and city rules that may affect your move, check the official source for your city and state.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as move-in cost?

First-month or prorated rent, the security deposit, pet and admin fees, and moving costs.

Is the deposit included in the first-month total?

Yes. The first-month cash needed includes the deposit and fees, and the total move-in cost adds moving costs on top.

Do deposit rules vary by state?

Yes. Check the Deposit calculator and the official source for your state; for states without a verified rule on this site, the answer is Check official source.
