Late Fee Checker — Oregon

Short answer

Oregon (ORS 90.260): a 4-day grace period, the fee must be in the written rental agreement, and the fee is 5% of periodic rent for each succeeding 5-day period, not a single flat 5% cap. Example: $2,000 rent paid 11 days after the due date = 7 days late after grace = 2 periods × $100 = $200.

Formula and counting convention

Grace end date = due date + 4 days. Rent is late on the next day (due date + 5 days).

Days late after grace = paid date − grace end date, if the paid date is after the grace end date; otherwise 0.

Counting convention — stated explicitly: each started 5-day period counts as a full period. Number of periods = ceiling(days late after grace ÷ 5). There is no silent rounding down: 1 day late after grace = 1 period; 5 days = 1 period; 6 days = 2 periods.

Fee due under this structure = number of periods × 5% × periodic rent.

Written-agreement requirement: under ORS 90.260 the fee must be in the written rental agreement. Check the lease's written fee term before relying on this calculation.

Worked examples

Example 1 — the required test: Rent $2,000, 5% per 5 days, paid 11 days after the due date. Grace end = due + 4 days, so days late after grace = 11 − 4 = 7 days. Periods = ceiling(7 ÷ 5) = 2 started periods. Fee = 2 × 5% × $2,000 = 2 × $100 = $200.

Example 2 — within grace: Rent $2,000, due 2026-10-01, paid 2026-10-04. Grace end = 2026-10-05. Paid on or before the grace end date, so days late after grace = 0 and fee under this structure = $0.

Example 3 — first period, then a second period starts: Rent $1,500, due 2026-10-01, paid 2026-10-11. Grace end = 2026-10-05. Days late after grace = 6. Periods = ceiling(6 ÷ 5) = 2. Fee = 2 × 5% × $1,500 = $150. Note that 5 days late after grace would be 1 period ($75); the 6th day starts the second period.

Example 4 — exactly one period: Rent $1,500, due 2026-10-01, paid 2026-10-10. Grace end = 2026-10-05. Days late after grace = 5. Periods = ceiling(5 ÷ 5) = 1. Fee = 1 × 5% × $1,500 = $75.

Limitations

  • Only Oregon's structure above is verified for this tool. All other states return “Check official source” with no fee amount.
  • Totals here follow the counting convention stated above, so they can exceed 5% of one month's rent if more periods elapse.
  • Daily and flat fee structures exist and are not calculated here; they depend on the written agreement terms.
  • The fee must be in the written rental agreement (ORS 90.260). If the lease has no such written term, do not rely on this calculation.
  • This tool does not decide whether rent was in fact late, whether payment was received on the date entered, or whether any other remedy applies.
  • Local ordinances may be stricter and may override state rules.

Frequently asked questions

What is Oregon's late fee grace period?

Under ORS 90.260 there is a 4-day grace period: rent due on day 1 is late on day 5.

Is Oregon's late fee a single flat 5% cap?

No. This tool does not present a single flat 5% overall cap as settled law. The 5% per 5-day period structure is one statutory structure; daily and flat structures also exist and depend on the written agreement terms.

Must the late fee be in the rental agreement?

Yes. Under ORS 90.260 the fee must be in the written rental agreement. Always check the lease's written fee term.
