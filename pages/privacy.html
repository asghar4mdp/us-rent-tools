Privacy Policy

US Rent Tools is designed to minimise data collection. The calculators on this site run client-side, in your browser. The figures you enter into a calculator are processed on your own device and are not sent to a server by the tools, and there are no accounts, sign-up, or login on this site.

What we do not collect through the tools

  • No calculator inputs are sent to a server by the tools.
  • No account, password, or profile information is collected, because there are no accounts.
  • No external JavaScript, fonts, or tracking are loaded by the tool pages.

What may still apply

A web host or content delivery service may record ordinary server logs, such as the page requested and the time of the request, as part of operating any website. Those logs, if any, are not calculator inputs and are not used by this site to identify individual users’ calculations. If a contact form is added at launch, the information you choose to send through that form will be handled as described on the contact page at that time, and this policy will be updated before the form goes live if its handling differs from what is stated here.

Links to other sites

Guides link to official sources and statutes. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them. This policy was last updated on 2026-10-25.
