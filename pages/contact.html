Contact

We welcome corrections, calculator suggestions, and questions about how a result was calculated. If you report a possible error, please include the page address, the figures you entered, the result you saw, and the official source you checked, so we can reproduce and verify the issue.

Contact form

A contact form will be provided here at launch. Contact details will be added at launch; no email address, phone number, or postal address is published on this page yet, and none should be inferred from any other page on this site. Until the form and contact details are added at launch, please do not send personal documents or sensitive information through any channel purporting to be this site unless you have verified it through the launched site itself.

What to include in a message

  • The page you were using and the date you used it.
  • The figures you entered and the result shown.
  • The official source you checked and what it says.
  • Whether you are reporting an error, suggesting a feature, or asking a general question.

We aim to verify reported errors against the official source and the test cases on the methodology page before changing a calculator or guide.
