What this means

The calculators and guides on US Rent Tools explain arithmetic and summarise verified state rules. They do not assess your lease, your property’s coverage or exemptions, the validity of a notice, or a local ordinance that may apply to you. A result that fits a state formula may still be unlawful for a particular home, and a result that exceeds a formula may still be lawful if an exemption or a different rule applies. Do not rely on this site alone for a decision with legal or financial consequences.

Get local advice when it matters

If a deadline is near, an eviction or payment notice is involved, an amount is large, or the facts are disputed, contact a local housing department, tenant or landlord organisation, or a qualified professional in your jurisdiction.
