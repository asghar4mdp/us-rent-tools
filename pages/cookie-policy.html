Cookie Protocol

Short answer: US Rent Tools does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or tracking cookies. The calculators and guides load no external scripts, fonts, or trackers.

What this site does — and does not — store

  • No advertising cookies: there are no ads on this site, and no advertising cookies are set.
  • No analytics or tracking cookies: the tools do not load third-party analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No account cookies: there are no accounts, sign-up, or login on this site, so no account or session cookies are needed for the tools.
  • Calculator inputs: figures you enter are processed in your own browser by the tool on the page. They are not sent to a server by the tools and are not stored as cookies by this site.
  • Your browser's own settings: your browser may still store ordinary local data for pages you visit, such as cached files, unless you clear it. That is controlled by your browser, not by this site.

Essential host and server logs, if any

A web host or content delivery service may record ordinary server logs, such as the page requested and the time of the request, as part of operating any website. Those logs, if any, are not calculator inputs, are not used by this site to identify individual users' calculations, and are not advertising data. If a form or support email is added at launch, this page will be updated before it goes live if its handling differs from what is stated here.

Third-party links

Guides link to official sources and statutes. Those sites have their own cookie and privacy policies, and this page does not cover them.
